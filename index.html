Test Locally & Deploy to Vercel

Zero backend, zero database, 100% free forever

Why your fiancée can join from anywhere:

This app connects using 3 redundant encrypted WebSocket relays (EMQX, HiveMQ, Mosquitto) + WebRTC simultaneously. As soon as you and your fiancée enter the same Room Code (like LOVE-2026), your browsers find each other in 1 second—even across different Wi-Fi or 4G/5G mobile data!

  1. Click Download index.html below to save this single file to a folder on your laptop.
  2. To test on your laptop first: Double-click the downloaded index.html in Chrome/Edge/Brave, enter a room code, and open a second tab with the same code!
  3. To deploy to Vercel: Push that folder containing index.html to GitHub and import it on vercel.com/new (or drag-and-drop the folder on app.netlify.com/drop).
  4. Send your Vercel link to your fiancée on WhatsApp/SMS—when she taps it and enters the Room Code, you're connected!
